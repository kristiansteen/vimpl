## 3. Cookies and browser storage

We use one cookie you cannot switch off, because sign-in does not work without it. Statistics cookies on vimpl.com and ailean.dk are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change that choice at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of each site, or <a href="#" data-cookie-settings>here for vimpl.com</a>. IP addresses are anonymised, and the data is not used for advertising. Fonts are served from our own servers, so loading a page sends nothing to Google.

| Name | Site | Type | Purpose | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| refreshToken | vimpl and Ailean apps | Necessary cookie | Keeps you signed in; cannot be read by scripts | 7 days, or when you sign out |
| vimpl_consent, ailean_consent | vimpl.com, ailean.dk | Browser storage | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you clear your browser data |
| _ga, _ga_G8B3MTE4X7, _ga_N6T4H0QH68 | vimpl.com, ailean.dk | Statistics cookie (Google Analytics), consent only | Counts visits and page views | 2 years |
| voice2bpmn_lang and app settings | Ailean app | Browser storage | Remembers your language, process taxonomy and logo choice | Until you clear your browser data |
| Saved flows | Ailean app | Browser storage | Keeps a copy of your work on this device so it opens fast and survives a lost connection | Until you sign out or clear your browser data |

The Ailean app keeps a copy of your work in your browser. Signing out deletes that copy, so sign out when you are done on a shared computer.

The booking link opens Google Calendar, and a video you add to a process opens from YouTube. Google's own privacy policy applies there.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy (art. 15);
- correct it (art. 16);
- delete it, including your whole account (art. 17); you can delete your own boards and flows in the app at any time;
- restrict how we use it (art. 18);
- receive it in a machine-readable format (art. 20);
- stop using it where we rely on legitimate interest, including onboarding e-mails (art. 21);
- withdraw consent, for example to website statistics, at any time; this does not affect use before you withdrew.

Write to [privacy@vimpl.com](mailto:privacy@vimpl.com). We answer within one month.

**Complaints.** You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, [www.datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk), or to the authority where you live or work.

**Changes.** We update this notice when our processing changes and show the date at the top. We tell account holders by e-mail about changes that matter to them.
