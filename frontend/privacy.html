Last updated: 24 November 2026.

1. Who we are and what this notice covers

vimpl.com A/S, CVR no. 41663073, Asger Rygs Gade 19, 3. tv., DK-1727 Copenhagen V, Denmark ("we") is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at privacy@vimpl.com.

This notice covers:

  • Our websites: vimpl.com and ailean.dk.
  • Your account in the vimpl and Ailean apps: signing up, signing in, billing and the e-mails we send you.

It does not cover the content you and your organisation put into the apps: boards, interviews, process descriptions, diagrams and plans. For that content your organisation is the controller and we process it on its behalf under our Data Processing Agreement. If you were invited to an Ailean interview or round, your organisation decides how your contribution is used; ask it first.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We use one cookie you cannot switch off, because sign-in does not work without it. Statistics cookies on vimpl.com and ailean.dk are set only if you accept them in the cookie banner. You can change that choice at any time via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of each site, or here for vimpl.com. IP addresses are anonymised, and the data is not used for advertising. Fonts are served from our own servers, so loading a page sends nothing to Google.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy (art. 15);
  • correct it (art. 16);
  • delete it, including your whole account (art. 17); you can delete your own boards and flows in the app at any time;
  • restrict how we use it (art. 18);
  • receive it in a machine-readable format (art. 20);
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interest, including onboarding e-mails (art. 21);
  • withdraw consent, for example to website statistics, at any time; this does not affect use before you withdrew.

Write to privacy@vimpl.com. We answer within one month.

Complaints. You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, www.datatilsynet.dk, or to the authority where you live or work.

Changes. We update this notice when our processing changes and show the date at the top. We tell account holders by e-mail about changes that matter to them.
